Naive Hybrid Permission-aware

flowchart TD
  Start[Retrieval need] --> MT{Multi-tenant or ACL'd data?}
  MT -->|yes| PA[Permission-aware: principal filter IN the query]
  MT -->|no| KW{Misses on IDs, codes, exact keywords?}
  KW -->|yes| HY[Hybrid: lexical + vector]

  HY --> M

Toy vs production-oriented (from the canonical architecture)

Toy: PDF → chunks → embeddings → vector search → LLM.

Production-oriented: malware scan, parse, classify, ACL on the chunk, permission filter, citation, output DLP, audit, telemetry, evaluation (reference-architecture.md).
