High-value cheat-sheets

Four pocket tables. They summarize taught pages; they do not add new domains or invent metrics.

Sheet Use when
RAG vs agent Someone wants “an agent over the docs”
MCP vs tools Someone wants MCP because it is fashionable
Eval Someone wants to ship on a few chats or a judge score
OWASP Top 10 map Someone wants a checklist without controls 
flowchart TD
  Q{Need a decision?} --> R[RAG vs agent]
  Q --> M[MCP vs tools]
  Q --> E[Eval pocket]
  Q --> S[OWASP map]
